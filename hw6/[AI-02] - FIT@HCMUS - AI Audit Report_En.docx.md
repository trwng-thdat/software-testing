## **5\. Conclusion — When should AI be used (or not)?**

AI was strongest where the task was bounded by evidence I could re-check: tracing `server.js` line by line, deriving equivalence partitions, and — most valuably — **refusing** to act. It declared "BVA not applicable" for six parameters rather than inventing length limits, left `«pending»` where execution had not happened, and corrected a false premise in my own prompt about where SEC-01–SEC-07 are defined. Neither error is visible on reading; both surfaced only when I re-added columns and re-traced promises. My rule: use AI to *generate and derive*, never to *confirm its own completeness*. Every count gets re-added by hand, every code citation gets opened, and every deferred item gets a named owner — otherwise it disappears.

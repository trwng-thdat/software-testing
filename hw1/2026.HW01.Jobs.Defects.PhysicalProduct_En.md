| Mistake No. | AI mindmap mistake                                                                | Why it is wrong                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Corrected version / source                                                                                           |
| ----------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|           1 | Placing "Debugging" under the "Test Execution" phase.                             | Debugging is a development activity, not a testing activity. Under ISTQB FL (v4.0) Section 1.1.2, testing finds defects, whereas debugging is the process of locating, analyzing, and fixing them.                                                                                 | Move "Debugging" to Development Activities. Source: ISTQB FL v4.0 Section 1.1.2.                                     |
|           2 | Showing Quality Assurance (QA) as a sub-branch of Quality Control (QC).           | QA is process-oriented and preventative (focused on process improvement), while QC and testing are product-oriented and detective (focused on checking quality). QA is not a sub-branch of QC; rather, QC is a part of Quality Management, and QA is focused on process assurance. | Position QA as a process-oriented quality assurance category, distinct from QC. Source: ISTQB FL v4.0 Section 1.2.2. |
|           3 | Listing "QA Tester" as a formal ISTQB role responsible for writing the Test Plan. | ISTQB FL recognizes two primary testing roles: Test Manager and Tester. There is no formal "QA Tester" role. Furthermore, Test Planning is the responsibility of the Test Manager, not the Tester.                                                                                 | Rename role to "Tester" (or "Test Manager" for planning). Source: ISTQB FL v4.0 Section 5.1.1.                       |
